What to Expect on Market Day

Six hours of creativity, trading and practical learning

Note: The detailed hour-by-hour activity and stage timetable is currently to be announced (TBA) by the organisers. Below is the general flow for families and participants.

  1. Step 1 · Arrival & Setup

    Check In & Arrange Your Stall

    Arrive with your parent or guardian, check in at the registration desk and set up your products, price signs and cash box before doors open at 10:00 AM.

  2. Step 2 · 10:00 AM Doors Open

    Morning Market Showcase

    Visitors explore all six Showcase Zones. Young moguls greet customers, explain how they made their products and complete real sales.

  3. Step 3 · Midday & Afternoon

    Peer Learning & Trading

    Participants rotate short breaks with family support, visit other young creators’ stalls and adjust displays as stock sells.

  4. Step 4 · 4:00 PM Close

    Count Earnings & Celebrate

    Market trading wraps up at 4:00 PM. Families pack down, count total sales versus material costs and celebrate every young creator.

Stall Preparation Checklist

Six essentials for a smooth Market Day

Questions about what products are suitable? Call the organising team on +263 771 222 748 or +263 783 116 564.

  • 01

    Kid-Made Products

    Products should be created, baked, assembled or designed by the young participants with age-appropriate adult safety help.

  • 02

    Clear US$ Price Tags

    Use clear, bold price cards ($1, $2, $3, $5) so visitors can browse easily and children can calculate totals quickly.

  • 03

    Cash Box & Small Change

    Bring a secure cash pouch or box and small notes/change. Official event payment methods are TBA by the organisers.

  • 04

    Food & Allergen Labels

    If selling baked goods or treats, wrap items hygienically and display a clear card listing ingredients (e.g., nuts, dairy, gluten).

  • 05

    Portable Table Display

    Whether stalls/tables are provided or brought by participants is TBA—prepare a tablecloth, banner and display stands.

  • 06

    Parent/Guardian Presence

    An adult family member or teacher remains responsible for supervision, encouragement and safety throughout the day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions from parents, young moguls & schools

Need direct confirmation? Send us an enquiry or call +263 771 222 748.

What ages can participate in Mini Moguls Market Day?
The event concept note defines three age groups covering ages 5–18: Junior Moguls (5–8), Young Moguls (9–13) and Senior Moguls (14–18). Note that the promotional poster states ages 6–18—final confirmation of whether 5-year-olds or 6-year-olds mark the starting age is pending with the organisers.
Where is the event taking place in Harare?
Mini Moguls Market Day takes place in Harare, Zimbabwe on Saturday, 12 December 2026 from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. The specific Harare venue is currently to be announced (TBA).
How much is the public entrance fee and stall registration fee?
The public entrance fee for visitors is US$5 per person, subject to final confirmation. Participant stall registration fees (if any) and the registration deadline are currently to be confirmed by the organisers.
Are tables, stalls or chairs provided?
Whether stalls, tables and display equipment are provided at the venue or brought by participants is currently to be confirmed by the organisers. We recommend planning a portable tabletop setup.
Can siblings, friends or school clubs share one stall?
Yes! Young creators can register as a solo entrepreneur or collaborate in a sibling, friend or school team stall.
Does a parent or legal guardian need to submit the registration?
Yes. A parent, legal guardian or authorised school educator must submit any stall registration on behalf of a child and remain involved in supervision on event day.
Is online payment collected on this website?
No. No online payment is collected on this website, and event-day payment methods are to be confirmed by the organisers.
