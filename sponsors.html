Invest in Harare’s next generation of young creators.

Mini Moguls Market Day brings children, teenagers, parents, schools and local businesses together on Saturday, 12 December 2026 (10:00 AM – 4:00 PM). Partnering with the market connects your organisation with families across Harare while directly supporting youth financial literacy, creativity and practical entrepreneurship.

Ways organisations & schools can partner

  • 1. Showcase Zone Co-Branding: Support one of the 6 Showcase Zones (*Arts, Crafts & Design*, *Baking, Food & Treats*, *Tech & Digital Innovation*, *Fashion & Accessories*, *Games & Interactive Fun*, or *Green & Sustainable Ideas*).
  • 2. Young Mogul Certificates & Encouragement Prizes: Celebrate creativity, customer service, display design and teamwork across Junior (5–8*), Young (9–13) and Senior (14–18) tracks.
  • 3. In-Kind Event Infrastructure: Provide gazebos, tables, signage printing, family seating or refreshments for Saturday, 12 December 2026.
  • 4. School & Youth Club Delegations: Register a group of student creators from your school or entrepreneurship club.
