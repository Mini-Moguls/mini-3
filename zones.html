    Official Age Groups & Formats

    How Mini Moguls adapts to every age group

    Age Discrepancy Note: The concept note specifies ages 5–18 across the three groups below, while the promotional poster specifies ages 6–18 (pending organiser confirmation).

    Junior Moguls

    Ages 5–8* · Guided Discovery (*5 vs 6 start TBA)

    An encouraging first taste of entrepreneurship! Junior Moguls create simple crafts, bracelets, painted bookmarks or sweet treats with close parent/guardian support at the stall.

    • • Simple $1–$5 price points
    • • Parent/guardian right beside the table
    • • Greeting customers with confidence
    • • Hands-on coin & note counting

    Junior Moguls Track · Ages 5–8*

    Young Moguls

    Ages 9–13 · Independent Creators

    Young Moguls take the lead in designing their product range, calculating material costs, building an eye-catching stall display and pitching directly to Harare families.

    • • Solo or sibling/friend co-founders
    • • Cost-per-unit & profit calculation
    • • Custom stall banners & packaging
    • • 20-second customer pitch practice

    Young Moguls Track · Ages 9–13

    Senior Moguls

    Ages 14–18 · Youth Ventures

    For teenagers ready to test real micro-enterprises—from custom streetwear, artisanal food brands and graphic design to coding demos and eco-friendly products.

    • • Brand identity & product packaging
    • • Margin, bundle & inventory planning
    • • Customer feedback & pre-orders
    • • Real-world youth enterprise skills

    Senior Moguls Track · Ages 14–18

    Team, Sibling & School Stalls

    Collaborative Youth Entrepreneurship

    Entrepreneurship is also about teamwork. Co-founders and school clubs can apply to run a shared stall, dividing responsibilities across production, display and sales.

    • • Sibling & friend co-founder stalls
    • • Primary & secondary school clubs
    • • Shared sales tally & role rotation
    • • Teacher or parent coordination
