About Mini Moguls Market Day

Where young creators become confident little moguls.

Mini Moguls Market Day is a dedicated children and youth entrepreneurship showcase in Harare, Zimbabwe on Saturday, 12 December 2026 (10:00 AM – 4:00 PM). Built around three words—CREATE. SELL. LEARN.—it gives young people a real marketplace to test their ideas, present products they have made, interact with customers and learn practical money skills.

The Young Mogul Journey

Entrepreneurship is best learned by doing—not just reading a textbook. Every participant moves through five practical stages from home preparation to Market Day trading.

Register a Young Mogul

  1. STAGE 01 · SPARK

    Brainstorming & Product Selection

    Young creators choose an original product, craft, baked treat, game or tech project suited to their interests and age track.

  2. STAGE 02 · CREATE

    Hands-On Production & Packaging

    With age-appropriate parent or teacher supervision, participants make their stock, design labels and prepare their stall name.

  3. STAGE 03 · PRICE

    Costing & Financial Literacy

    Children add up what their materials cost, set fair US$ prices and practise counting change before event day.

  4. STAGE 04 · SELL

    Market Day Showcase (Sat, 12 Dec 2026)

    From 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in Harare (Venue TBA), young moguls run their stalls, greet visitors and make real sales.

  5. STAGE 05 · LEARN

    Reflection, Profit & Confidence

    After the market closes, families review what sold best, calculate net profit after costs, and celebrate the skills gained.

Core Pillars · CREATE. SELL. LEARN.

Six skills every young entrepreneur practices at Market Day

Mini Moguls Market Day is designed to feel joyful and welcoming for children while teaching real-world capabilities valued by parents, educators and business leaders.

  • 1. Creativity & Innovation

    Turning an imagination spark into a tangible product, food item, game or service that other people want to buy.

  • 2. Financial Awareness

    Understanding material costs, pricing, customer value, counting change and the difference between revenue and profit.

  • 3. Confident Communication

    Looking customers in the eye, explaining how a product was made, answering questions politely and saying thank you.

  • 4. Problem-Solving

    Adapting table displays, restocking popular items and making thoughtful decisions throughout the market day.

  • 5. Collaboration & Teamwork

    Working alongside siblings, friends, classmates and parents to divide tasks and support fellow young sellers.

  • 6. Ownership & Pride

    Experiencing the pride of earning money through personal effort, creativity and honest workmanship.
